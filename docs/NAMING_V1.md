# Khela — v1 Name Recommendation

*Respects the locked decision: the brand root stays **Khela** (Bengali খেলা = "game/play"). The
research validates Khela as a warm, on-meaning, ownable root — but found a serious collision that
means **do not ship the bare word "Khela."** Use a distinctive compound. Findings + recommendation
below. Availability is directional — re-verify live; lawyer clears the final mark (CLAUDE.md #4).*

---

## 0. The headline finding (why bare "Khela" can't be the store name)

**"Khela" is already a live real-money gambling brand in your exact market.** `khela.app` bills
itself as *"Bangladesh's Premier Online Gaming & Betting Platform"* — 500K+ players, bKash/Nagad
deposits, sports betting, slots, **and "Single Deck Blackjack."** Same word, same country, same
audience, **same headline game (blackjack)**, real cashout. Plus **Khela88** and **Khelo24Bet**
(cricket-betting + casino) and multiple **"Khela Hobe / Khelo / +number"** betting/"earn-money" apps.

This is a quadruple problem for shipping bare "Khela":
- **Legal positioning:** your entire defense is "non-cashable social game, NOT gambling." Sharing a
  name with a Bangladeshi *betting* site that offers blackjack actively undermines that — exactly
  backwards from the CLAUDE.md dual-currency firewall.
- **Trademark:** "Khela" = "game" for a game app is **generic-to-descriptive** — weak/unregistrable
  on its own (like trademarking "Game"). A distinctive compound is registrable and enforceable.
- **Store policy:** Apple 4.3 (saturated-category clone) + impersonation risk rises when name +
  concept resemble an existing gaming app.
- **Discovery + trust:** bare "Khela" sits next to grey-market betting brands in search.

**Conclusion:** keep **Khela** as the root (decision respected), add a **distinctive, playful,
non-money second word** that (a) disambiguates from the betting brands, (b) adds trademark strength,
and (c) reads as *fun social play, not betting*.

Also: always keep the **"h"** ("Khela," never "Kela" — "kela" = "banana" in Hindi / crude slang in
Assamese). And avoid money-loaded second words entirely: no Bet, 24, 365, Win, Cash, Jeet, Earn, Taka.

## 1. Linguistic validation (the good news)
- **Bengali:** "Khela" = the everyday noun "game/play" — casual, warm, instantly legible. ✓
- **Hindi/Urdu:** the clean noun is "khel"; "khela" reads as "played" / game-ish — slightly off-
  register but clearly in the play field, not negative. ✓ (good enough pan-regionally)
- **"Khela Hobe"** ("game on") is a viral Bengali sport/political rallying cry — it has supercharged
  familiarity and "game-on" energy for the root. Use the *word* Khela (benefits from the buzz);
  **don't** use the full phrase "Khela Hobe" as a tagline (live political slogan).
- **Pronunciation:** KHEH-la (not KAY-la); all-ASCII, 5 letters, easy to type/search. ✓
- **Faint undertone:** "khela" also carries a "trickery / twist of fate" secondary sense and "khel"
  brushes gambling slang — minor, and arguably on-theme; just lean on "play/fun" in copy.

## 2. Recommended brand name (the compound)

Ranked, with the reasoning:

1. **Khela Adda** ⭐ *(top pick — best fit for a social card game)*
   - *Adda* = the quintessentially Bengali ritual of friends gathering for long, joyful, informal
     hangout (in the OED since 2004). It maps **perfectly** onto a multiplayer card *table = a social
     hangout* — the exact emotional core of a social casino. Warm, unmistakably Bengali, ownable.
   - **Caveat to verify:** a real-money brand **"Khiladi Adda"** exists (Ludo/Rummy). Different first
     word (Khela ≠ Khiladi), but confirm no confusion/standing mark before locking.

2. **Khela Tash** *(cleanest disambiguation)*
   - *Tash* (তাস / ताश) = "playing cards." Signals **cards and play, not betting** — the sharpest
     distance from the "Khela = betting" brands. Distinctive, native, fresh (few/no apps use it).
     Slight redundancy with a "Cards" keyword in the title, but strong as a brand.

3. **Khela Club** *(safe, modern, international)*
   - "Club" = members/lounge/social, internationally legible, low collision. The dependable choice
     if you want broad pan-South-Asian + diaspora reach over a Bengali-specific word.

4. **Khela Mahal** *(premium alternative)* — *mahal* = "palace" (premium connotation, pan-Hindi/Urdu/
   Bengali; "Game Palace"). Richer/more aspirational than the clichéd "Gold."

**Avoid:** bare **Khela**, **Khelo**, **Khela World** (generic + namespace adjacent to betting/
fantasy brands), **Khela Gold** (clichéd; "Gold" is others' qualifier), **Khela Games** (redundant —
"game games").

My single recommendation: **Khela Adda** for the social/Bengali fit, with **Khela Tash** as the
strongest alternate if the Khiladi-Adda check or the diaspora-legibility of "adda" gives you pause.

## 3. Store title structure (ASO — separate from the brand)

The **brand** is the identity ("Khela Adda"). The **store title** anchors the brand in position 1
and appends the highest-value keyword for discovery — because at launch nobody searches "Khela," so
a keyword-forward title is correct early.

- **Hero keyword = "Teen Patti"** — by far the highest-volume, highest-intent South-Asian search
  term (the whole clone ecosystem fights over it). Blackjack/slots have a fraction of that demand.
- **Don't make the brand "Teen Patti <x>"** — undifferentiated, Apple-4.3-risky, and it can't outlive
  the lead game when poker/roulette ship. A distinct brand that *carries* the keyword is durable.

**Google Play title (30-char limit — the heaviest keyword field):**
> **`Khela Adda: Teen Patti & Cards`** — 30 chars ✓

(Alternates: `Khela Tash: Teen Patti & Cards` = 30 ✓ · `Khela Club: Teen Patti, Rummy` = 29 ✓)

**Google Play short description (80 chars, indexed) — pack keywords + the compliance signal:**
> `Play Teen Patti, Blackjack & Slots. Free social card games — for fun, no real money.`

**Apple:** Name (30) = same as Play · Subtitle (30) = `Blackjack, Slots & Poker Fun` (adds keywords,
no repeats) · Keyword field (100, comma-sep, no spaces, no repeats):
`casino,3patti,flush,rummy,holdem,3card,patti,taas,bengali,multiplayer,chips,cards`
→ put "casino" in the **keyword field, not the title** (ranks for it at lower age-rating/region drag).

**Migration:** v1 keyword-forward (above) → once the brand earns searches, shift toward
`Khela Adda – Card & Casino Games` and demote game keywords into the subtitle/short description.

**Hard rules:** ≤30 chars; no ALL CAPS / emojis / "#1/Best/Free" in title (Google metadata policy);
never put another app's brand in the title (Gold/Master/Octro/Zupee/MPL) — only generic game nouns
(teen patti, blackjack, slots, poker, cards are all safe public-domain terms); carry the three
social-casino disclaimers (adult audience / no real money / no prizes) in the description.

## 4. Clearance checklist (before locking — must do live)
1. **Trademark search:** IP India (the relevant jurisdiction for a Bengali word) + USPTO/WIPO for the
   chosen compound; confirm no live "Khela Adda"/"Khela Tash" mark and assess the Khiladi-Adda overlap.
2. **Store search** (US **and** BD/IN storefronts) on Google Play + App Store for the exact compound.
3. **Domain WHOIS:** grab the matching `.com`/`.gg`/`.io` (avoid anything near `khela.app`).
4. **Social-handle sweep:** IG / X / TikTok / YouTube / FB for the compound.
5. **Lawyer clears the final mark** + the BD/IN non-cashable framing (already mandated).

**One-line answer:** keep **Khela**, ship it as **Khela Adda** (brand), store-titled
**`Khela Adda: Teen Patti & Cards`** — distinctive enough to clear the betting-brand collision, warm
and on-culture, keyword-forward for discovery. Verify availability + trademark live before locking.
